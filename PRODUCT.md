# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Students and academics who need a smart, reliable filing cabinet for their notes and study materials. Their context involves scattered information across multiple apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, email) and the need to quickly retrieve specific documents across devices.

## Product Purpose
Quire serves as an academic sanctuary, allowing users to collect notes from anywhere and find them instantly via full-text search. It syncs securely via Google Drive to ensure they never lose a file.

## Brand Personality
Academic Sanctuary, disciplined, clean, warm, professional, modern.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS, typical cold/clinical productivity tools, flashy/overstimulating designs, emoji-heavy layouts.

## Design Principles
- Keep the academic feel quiet but high-end.
- Favor subtle, premium micro-animations over aggressive motion.
- Ensure professional, minimalist iconography over emojis.
- Emphasize editorial typography and deliberate spacing for a scholarly rhythm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast for legibility, support light/dark modes for eye strain reduction, and respect reduced motion preferences.
