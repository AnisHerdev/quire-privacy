Zero Effort

Magic Auto-Sorter

Never waste time organizing. Quire looks at your filename (like CS201_HW4.pdf), instantly recognizes the subject code, matches it to your existing folders, and pre-fills tags for you—all offline and privately on your device. Just confirm and it files itself!

  • Smart Reading: Automatically picks up subject codes like CS, LIT, or MATH from your shared files.
  • Folder Matching: Suggests matching folders in your library so you don't have to search.
  • Auto-Tagging: Pre-fills your custom tags instantly so they are ready to file in one tap.

💡 Tips for Testers

  • Google Drive Permission: Quire operates as an academic sanctuary, syncing notes privately to your own Google Account. Please grant the Google Drive permission when the app starts.
  • No 404 Errors: If you get a "404 App not available" error on the Play Store, make sure you completed Step 1 and are signed into the correct Google Account in your browser.
  • Collect Notes Anywhere: Try sharing a PDF or note from WhatsApp or Telegram directly to Quire via the OS share sheet.
