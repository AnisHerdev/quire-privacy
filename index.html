Features

📥

Zero-Friction Intake

Share files from WhatsApp, Telegram, or any app directly into Quire via the OS share sheet. Files land in your Inbox automatically.

🗂️

Smart Organization

Auto-detect subjects from filenames. Organize into semesters and sub-folders. Batch move files with undo support.

🔍

Full-Text Search

BM25-ranked search across all your PDFs and documents. Find notes by content, not just filename — even offline.

☁️

Cross-Device Sync

Your notes sync across all your devices via Google Drive. File on your phone, read on your laptop.

🔒

Privacy by Design

All text extraction and processing happens on your device. No cloud functions, no external servers. You own your data.

📴

Offline First

Cache files locally. Read PDFs and search your notes without an internet connection.

Built for Privacy

Quire processes all your documents on your device. No cloud text extraction, no third-party analytics, no data selling. Your Google Drive is yours — Quire only accesses its own folder.
